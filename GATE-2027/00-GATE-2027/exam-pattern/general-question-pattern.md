## Question types (Information Brochure section 7.2.1)

GATE 2027 test papers contain three types of questions.

**Multiple Choice Question (MCQ).** Carries 1 or 2 marks. Four answers, of which only one is correct. A wrong answer chosen in an MCQ has negative marking: 1/3 of a mark for a 1-mark MCQ, and 2/3 of a mark for a 2-mark MCQ.

**Multiple Select Question (MSQ).** Carries 1 or 2 marks. Four answers, of which one or more than one is correct. There is no negative marking for a wrong answer. There is no partial mark for choosing a partially correct combination or any single wrong choice. Full marks are given only when the correct combination is selected.

**Numerical Answer Type (NAT).** Carries 1 or 2 marks. The answer is a signed real number entered with the virtual numeric keypad. The computer keyboard is disabled. No choices are shown. The answer can be an integer, such as 10 or −10, or a decimal, such as 10.1, 10.01, or −10.001. The question indicates the number of decimal places. Candidates are advised to round off only at the end of the calculation and not at intermediate stages. There is no negative marking for a wrong answer, and there is no partial marking.

Brochure section 4 also states: “There is no partial marking for any question.” The Question Paper Pattern page states “There is no partial marking in MSQ” and does not use the phrase “for any question” in that cell.

## Abilities tested (Information Brochure section 7.2.2)

- **Recall:** questions based on facts, principles, formulae, or laws. The answer is expected from memory or at most a short computation.
- **Comprehension:** understanding of basic concepts and simple conclusions from fundamental ideas.
- **Application:** applicability of knowledge through computation or logical reasoning.
- **Analysis and Synthesis:** data, diagrams, images, and similar material that require analysis. A synthesis question might require comparison of two or more pieces of information. Questions in this category could involve recognising unstated assumptions or separating useful information from irrelevant information.

The questions may be a mix of a single standalone statement/phrase/data based questions, a combination of options, or match-the-given-items type.

## Examination conduct (Information Brochure sections 7 and 7.1, and the FAQ page)

- The examination is a computer-based test. Questions are shown on a computer screen.
- A virtual scientific calculator is available on the screen. Candidates must use only that calculator. A physical calculator is not permitted.
- Calculators, mobile phones, bluetooth devices, electronic/communication devices, wallets, books, papers, loose sheets, data or handbooks, tables, pen or pencil boxes/pouches, and analog/digital/smart watches are prohibited in the exam halls. Possession leads to disqualification whether or not the item is used. If a candidate is found to be involved in malpractice, the GATE committee reserves the right to ban the candidate from appearing in GATE for the next 3 years. GATE 2027 will not be responsible for safeguarding personal belongings. The code of conduct is Appendix C of the Information Brochure.
- Scribble pads are provided for rough work. The candidate writes name and registration number on the pad before using it. Only one scribble pad may be possessed at a time. The first pad must be returned before a second pad is taken. All scribble pads must be returned before leaving the hall.
- Candidates may occupy allotted seats 40 minutes before the scheduled start. They can log in and start reading the instructions 20 minutes before the start. Login is not permitted after 15 minutes from the scheduled start. Candidates are not permitted to leave the examination hall before the scheduled end. A permitted delayed entry does not add extra time.
- Examination for some test papers may be held in multiple sessions. A candidate appears in only one session, as printed on the admit card.
- Brochure highlights: GATE 2027 examination dates are 6, 7, 13, 14, 20, and 21 February 2027, in forenoon and afternoon sessions. Dates are liable to change. The current date table is in `../important-information/important-dates.md`.

## Compensatory time

Two official wordings are published. Both cite O.M. F. No. P-13013/75/2023-Policy-DD-III dated 1 August 2025.

Question Paper Pattern page:

> A PwD candidate, in the category of blindness, locomotor disability (both arms only) and cerebral palsy, can request for the compensatory time of one hour upon production of valid disability certificate/UDID card. In case of all other specified disabilities the compensatory time will be allowed on production of a certificate to the effect that the person concerned has a functional limitation to write, caused by the specified disability. Please refer to Appendix-B1.

Information Brochure section 4:

> PwD candidates are eligible for compensatory time of 20 minutes per hour as per the guidelines F. No. P-13013/75/2023-Policy-DD-III dated August 01, 2025 of Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment. Following the same guidelines, the candidates will have to submit additional documents. Please refer to Appendix B for more details.

Information Brochure section 7.1:

> All test papers of GATE 2027 will be of three hours duration (a maximum of four hours in case of candidates availing compensatory time).

The FAQ page states that on-spot requests for compensatory time or a scribe at the examination hall will not be entertained, and that dyslexic candidates who avail a scribe are covered by compensatory (extra) time of one hour. Details are in `../important-information/faq.md`.
