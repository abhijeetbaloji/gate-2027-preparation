## Data required while filling the application

Registration for GATE 2027 through DigiLocker is mandatory for all Indian nationals. The Required Documents page states that the necessary data for filling the application will be fetched from DigiLocker: name, date of birth, mobile number, email id, profile picture, address, and verified ID card number.

The page says: “Please create/update your VERIFIED DigiLocker account NOW!”

GATE aspirants have an option of registering through an unverified DigiLocker account. They can select the default ID proof during application filling. These applications will be scrutinized after the registration deadline for necessary documents.

Consent: the candidate provides consent to GATE 2027 to access data in DigiLocker.

Personal details on the Required Documents page:

- Name in the application must be exactly the same as in the valid photo ID, which must be produced in original at the centre. The GATE 2027 scorecard will be issued as per the name fetched from DigiLocker.
- Date of birth is fetched from the DigiLocker ID. Change in date of birth is not allowed during the application process.
- DigiLocker-verified mobile number and verified email ID will be used for the application.
- Address for communication, including PIN code, will be fetched from DigiLocker.
- Name and mobile number of a parent or guardian should be provided additionally.
- Details of the degree.
- Institute / college name with PIN code.
- Choice(s) of GATE test paper(s).
- Six GATE examination cities from the same zone.
- Internet-banking, debit card, credit card, UPI, or wallet details for fee payment.

Notification dated 1 August 2026: registration through DigiLocker is mandatory for all Indian nationals.

Notification dated 19 September 2026: candidates are advised not to delete their verified or unverified DigiLocker accounts after application submission and payment of the application fee. Future communications will be made only with respect to the DigiLocker account used to submit the application.

Notification dated 6 September 2026: a verified DigiLocker account means the profile is verified through e-KYC. Creating a DigiLocker account does not by itself make it a verified account.

Notification dated 10 September 2026: if there is a data mismatch between DigiLocker and the GATE 2027 portal, complete e-KYC in DigiLocker and try again.

## Documents required

From the Required Documents page:

- The candidate’s live photo will be captured during registration. Use a computer with a webcam, or a phone with a minimum 5 MP front-facing camera. Capture the face image in a well-lit area.
- A good-quality image of the candidate’s photograph, conforming to the specifications.
- A good-quality image of the candidate’s signature, conforming to the specifications.
- The same original ID that was verified from DigiLocker must be produced at the GATE 2027 examination centre.
- Category (SC/ST) certificate, if applicable, in PDF, uploaded to DigiLocker. Create a folder named `GATE2027`. Upload the category certificate as `CATEGORY.pdf`.
- Scanned copy of UDID (preferred) or PwD certificate, if applicable, in PDF, uploaded in the `GATE2027` folder. Name the file `UDID_CERT.pdf` or `PWD_CERT.pdf`.
- Scanned copy of Appendix-B1 or Appendix-B2 or the dyslexic certificate, whichever is applicable, compiled with the PwD certificate as a single PDF and uploaded in the same drive as `PWD_CERT.pdf`.
- Certificate required for compensatory time and scribe.

Ensure that uploaded documents are readable and clear. Unreadable documents may delay processing and may lead to rejection of candidature.

Notification dated 17 September 2026: each file is uploaded to the DigiLocker Drive under the `GATE2027` folder with the specified file name. The file size should be between 10 KB and 600 KB.

## FAQ on what is uploaded

- General, OBC-NCL, and EWS candidates: no documents are required in the `GATE2027` DigiLocker folder. The folder name is without a space.
- SC / ST candidates: only the category certificate, file name `CATEGORY.pdf`.
- PwD candidates: documents as specified on page 30 of the Information Brochure, using `PWD_CERT.pdf`, `UDID_CERT.pdf`, and, if required, Appendix-B1 or Appendix-B2 compiled with the PwD certificate and uploaded as `PWD_CERT.pdf`.
- No other documents are required in that folder.
- 10th, 12th, UG, and PG marksheets are not required in DigiLocker for GATE 2027 registration. Academic documents are not required during the examination process.
- The photograph in Aadhaar / DigiLocker is not used for the GATE 2027 application. The live photo and the recent photo uploaded during the application are used.
- If name or date of birth does not match across DigiLocker documents, the candidate can choose one of the verified documents DigiLocker displays, such as Aadhaar, PAN, Driving Licence, or APAAR ID.
- OBC-NCL and EWS certificates are not uploaded during the application, according to the FAQ. An SC/ST/PwD category certificate is required to avail the fee concession.

## Photo identity accepted

FAQ, for Indian candidates: Aadhaar-UID (preferable), Passport, PAN Card, Voter ID, and Driving License.

FAQ, for foreign nationals: a valid passport, any other valid photo ID issued by their government, or a driving license.

Information Brochure section 6.4 lists accepted photo IDs as Aadhaar-UID, Aadhaar Virtual ID, Passport, PAN card, Voter ID card, Driving License, and any valid photo ID issued by the Central or State Government. The same original valid photo ID must be produced at the examination. The brochure says candidates must upload the photo ID that has been issued most recently.

The admit-card FAQ states that a photocopy, a scanned copy, or an expired document is not accepted at the centre, and that a soft copy of the admit card on a phone, tablet, or laptop is not sufficient. A hard copy of the admit card, preferably printed in colour, is required, together with the original photo ID specified in the application.

## Photograph specifications

The GATE 2027 admit card and score card will contain the photograph submitted at the time of application.

- Upload a good-quality colour photograph of passport size (3.5 cm width × 4.5 cm height), with the face covering 60–70% of the photograph.
- Background must be white and must not contain any other objects or persons.
- The photograph must show the frontal face view looking directly into the camera, and must show the forehead, eyes, nose, and chin.
- The face must not be covered with caps, hats, sunglasses, coloured glasses, or similar objects. Normal spectacles for vision correction are allowed. A photograph with glare on the glasses is not acceptable. If the glare cannot be avoided, the spectacles must be removed.
- JPEG/JPG format. Minimum aspect ratio 0.66 and maximum aspect ratio 0.89. After cropping, the face must cover 60–70% of the image.
- Maximum pixel resolution 530 × 690. Minimum resolution 200 × 260. File size minimum 5 kB and maximum 600 kB.
- The face must be clearly visible and not covered by cloth or shadow.
- Photographs that do not meet the criteria could lead to rejection of the application, and the application fee will not be refunded.
- Head coverings are not permitted except for religious reasons. Facial features from the bottom of the chin to the top of the forehead, and both edges of the face, must be clearly shown.
- The page’s editing instruction: face should cover A2, A3, B2, B3, C2, and C3 grids; background should be plain and white; eyes and face should be towards the camera.

The page shows samples of acceptable photographs and samples that are not acceptable, including wrong size, sunglasses, shadow, improper background, face and head covered, other people, blur, glare on spectacles, a mask, a cap, objects in the background, and not looking straight into the camera.

## Signature specifications

- JPEG/JPG. Aspect ratio (height : width) of 1 : R, where R is between 2.75 and 3.75. After cropping, the signature must cover 70–80% of the image.
- Black or dark blue ink only.
- File size minimum 3 kB and maximum 300 kB.
- Maximum pixel resolution 580 × 180. Minimum resolution 250 × 80.
- The signature must be signed only by the applicant.
- If the signature does not match the signature at the examination, the applicant could be disqualified.

The page’s samples of signatures that are not acceptable include a dark background, all-capital letters, digital signatures, ink other than blue or black, a signature too small to view, initials, a signature that is not clearly visible, background or write-up, and a signature outside the box or not signed horizontally.

## If the upload does not meet the specification

The FAQ states that a photograph or signature that was not uploaded as specified makes the application defective. The candidate is informed by email, SMS, or phone from the respective zone and may rectify the defect until a deadline. Beyond that deadline the application will be considered provisional or rejected.

The same defective-and-rectify process is stated for an exchanged photograph and photo ID, and for a mismatch between the uploaded photo ID and the ID number entered.
