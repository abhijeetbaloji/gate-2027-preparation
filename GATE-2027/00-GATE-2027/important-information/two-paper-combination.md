A candidate fills only one application. A second paper, if chosen, is added in that application. The Test Papers & Syllabus page states that if there are multiple applications, only one will be accepted and the remaining applications will be rejected without any refund of the paid fee.

The secondary paper must be chosen from the allowed list for the primary paper. The order below is the order on the Two-Paper Combinations page.

## CS and DA, as published

- If the primary paper is **CS**, the secondary paper codes are DA, EC, GE, MA, ME, PH, RA, and ST.
- If the primary paper is **DA**, the secondary paper codes are CS, EC, EE, MA, ME, PH, RA, ST, and XE.
- CS appears as an allowed secondary paper for DA, EC, GE, MA, ME, PH, RA, and ST.
- DA appears as an allowed secondary paper for CS, EC, EE, MA, ME, PH, RA, ST, and XE.

Information Brochure Table 4 lists the same sets of codes. Some rows are printed in a different order. For example, the brochure prints the CS secondary papers as DA, EC, GE, MA, PH, RA, ST, ME, and the DA secondary papers as CS, EC, EE, MA, ME, PH, RA, ST, XE.

## Official notes on the Two-Paper Combinations page

- The test papers will be in English.
- Candidates opting to appear in two test papers must have a primary choice of test paper. The secondary test paper must be chosen from the allowed combinations.
- Combinations other than the listed ones are not allowed in the initial (regular and extended) windows of registration.
- GATE may open additional two-paper combinations at a later date. Any such opening will be notified on the GATE 2027 website. This is subject to the availability of infrastructure and scheduling feasibility.
- The examination centre for the secondary paper may be different from the centre for the primary paper, but in the same city, because of infrastructure and scheduling constraints. GATE 2027 shall not be liable for any legal obligations arising out of this issue.
- Under unforeseen circumstances, certain combinations may be removed from the list at a later date. In such cases, the fee paid towards the second test paper will be refunded.
