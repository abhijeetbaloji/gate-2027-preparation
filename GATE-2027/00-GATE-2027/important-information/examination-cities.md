## Choice of city

A candidate can opt for six cities from the list. All six options must be from the same GATE 2027 zone.

GATE 2027 reserves the right to add a new city or remove an existing one, and to allot a city that may not be from any of the options provided by the candidate.

The FAQ states that a candidate will most likely be allotted a centre in the city of their first choice, and that the other five choices become relevant when too many candidates opt for a city as their first choice.

## International centres

GATE 2027 will be conducted only in centres within India. It will not be conducted in centres outside India.

Foreign nationals and Indian citizens residing abroad may appear for GATE 2027 by paying the full application fee (₹2000 per test paper), but they must choose examination cities within India. They must make their own arrangements for travel and accommodation to appear at an assigned centre within India.
