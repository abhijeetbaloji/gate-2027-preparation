Amounts below are in Indian rupees, as shown with the rupee sign on the Application Fees page.

## Fee table on the Application Fees page

| Category | Regular period, one paper | Regular period, two papers | Extended period, one paper | Extended period, two papers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Female / SC / ST / PwD* candidates | ₹1000 | ₹2000 | ₹1500 | ₹2500 |
| All other candidates, including foreign nationals | ₹2000 | ₹4000 | ₹2500 | ₹4500 |

\* The Application Fees page states: “PwD means Persons with Benchmark Disability.”

The two-paper amounts on this page are twice the one-paper amounts.

## Same per-paper amounts in the Information Brochure

Brochure Table 5 is headed “Application fee per test paper.”

| Category | Regular period | Extended period |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Female / SC / ST / PwD* candidates | ₹1000 | ₹1500 |
| All other candidates, including foreign nationals | ₹2000 | ₹2500 |

Brochure statements attached to this table:

- For two papers, the fee payable is twice that of a single paper.
- The application fee does not include service charges, processing fees, and any other charges that the banks may levy.
- PwD here refers to Persons with Benchmark Disability. Only those candidates with benchmark disability (40% and higher) are eligible for fee concession. For benchmark disabilities, the brochure says to refer to the RPwD Act 2016.
- Dates are subject to change.
- The application fee is not transferable. It is not refundable except when a certain two-paper combination is removed.
- There are no international centres for GATE 2027. Foreign nationals or Indian nationals who wish to appear must travel to India.
- Payments must be made online using internet banking, debit card, credit card, wallet, or UPI. Candidates are advised to ensure that the e-commerce transaction facility is enabled on their credit or debit cards.

The date ranges printed in the Table 5 header are recorded in `important-dates.md`. The ranges that match the dates not struck through on the Important Dates page are regular registration from 2 September 2026 through 5 October 2026, and extended registration closing on 12 October 2026.

## Refunds

Notification dated 7 September 2026:

> The GATE 2027 registration fee is non-refundable. Refunds will be processed only for the duplicate transactions upon submission of valid proof. Unpaid accounts / accounts where transactions are not available will be deleted after the GATE 2027 registration portal closes.

The Two-Paper Combinations page states that if a listed combination is later removed, the fee paid towards the second test paper will be refunded.

The FAQ states that withdrawing an application does not refund the application fee. Extra payments received by GATE 2027 for one application are returned to the same bank account; the FAQ says this may take about fifteen working days. If an amount is debited but GATE 2027 has not acknowledged the payment, the FAQ says the money will be credited back, and that this may take about fifteen working days.

## Category and nationality notes from the FAQ

- The fee for foreign nationals is the full application fee.
- GATE 2027 is conducted only at centres within India. Foreign nationals and Indian citizens residing abroad pay the full application fee and choose examination cities within India. The Examination Cities page states that fee as ₹2000 per test paper.
- An OBC (non-creamy layer) or EWS candidate does not upload the category certificate for the application. The application fee for EWS candidates is the same as that for General candidates.
- An SC / ST / PwD candidate uploads the category certificate to avail the concession in application fee.

## Changes after submission

The FAQ states that, only after the last date for application submission, a request to change the GATE examination paper, the examination city, or the category will be entertained against additional payment. It says the dates for that window will be announced later, and that a request after that date will not be considered.

A second paper from the allowed combinations can be added to the same enrolment ID after payment. A new enrolment ID should not be created for that purpose.

## What is not recorded as a published fee

Those amounts are not copied here, because they are not published page content. The official FAQ confirms that an additional payment applies to later changes, and that the dates will be announced later. It does not state the amount on the FAQ page.
