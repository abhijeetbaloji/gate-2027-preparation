## Application process

**Multiple DigiLocker accounts on one mobile number.** DigiLocker asks which account to use. If both are unverified, verify at least one on the DigiLocker website, then continue on the GATE 2027 portal with the verified account. If one is verified and one is not, continue with the verified account. A verified account is marked with a blue verification checkmark.

**Documents in the `GATE2027` DigiLocker folder.** General, OBC-NCL, and EWS: none. SC/ST: only `CATEGORY.pdf`. PwD: the documents specified on page 30 of the Information Brochure, using `PWD_CERT.pdf`, `UDID_CERT.pdf`, and Appendix-B1 or Appendix-B2 compiled with the PwD certificate when required. No other documents go in that folder.

**Aadhaar or DigiLocker photograph.** The photo on Aadhaar or in DigiLocker is not used. The live photo and the recent photo uploaded during the application are used.

**Name or date-of-birth mismatch.** DigiLocker can be used to choose a verified document such as Aadhaar, PAN, Driving Licence, or APAAR ID. Details in the selected photo ID should be correct before submission. A name change on the scorecard after release requires the name-correction documents stated in the procedure. Admission is not under the purview of the organizing institute.

**Address and date of birth.** Both are fetched from DigiLocker / Aadhaar during registration.

**Marksheets.** 10th, 12th, UG, and PG marksheets are not uploaded for registration.

**Third-year B.A. / B.Com. / B.Sc., and third-year B.E. after diploma lateral entry.** Yes. An undergraduate student currently in the 3rd year or higher of a government-approved program in Engineering, Technology, Architecture, Science, Commerce, Arts, or Humanities is eligible.

**Age limit.** There is no age limit.

**Number of attempts.** A candidate may appear any number of times.

**Choice of paper.** A candidate may choose any paper, up to two from the given combinations, and should select a paper appropriate to the discipline of the qualifying degree.

**How to apply.** Use the application-portal link on the official website. It becomes active when registration opens.

**One email address.** One email address can be used for only one application form.

**Two papers.** Both papers are selected in a single form, from the given combinations. A second paper can be added to the same enrolment ID after payment. A new enrolment ID should not be created for the second paper.

**Six cities.** The first-choice city is the likely allotment. The other five choices matter when too many candidates choose the same first city. GATE reserves the right to add or remove a city and to allot a city outside the options given.

**Connection failure during submission.** Log in again and continue.

**OBC-NCL or EWS certificate upload.** No.

**SC/ST/PwD certificate.** Yes, to avail the fee concession.

**Fee payment.** The payment option appears after the form is filled. Listed payment options may be used. Service charges, processing fees, and bank charges are borne by the candidate. Two-paper fees are on the Application Fees page. Foreign nationals pay the full application fee. There are no centres outside India.

**Payment failure or a double debit.** Log in and check the payment status. If GATE did not receive the payment, a fresh payment may be initiated. An amount debited without an acknowledgement is credited back; the FAQ says this may take about fifteen working days. Extra payments for one application are returned to the same bank account on the same timescale.

**Printout.** The printout is not sent to the organizing institute or to any zonal office. It can be downloaded later by logging in with the enrolment ID and password.

**Status.** Check by logging in to the application portal.

**Changes after the PDF is generated.** Data cannot be modified after the online process is completed through PDF generation. A later window, against additional payment, is described for paper, city, and category changes. The FAQ says those dates will be announced later.

**Photograph, signature, or photo-ID defects.** The application is marked defective. The zone informs the candidate by email, SMS, or phone. Rectification is allowed until a deadline. After that, the application is provisional or rejected.

**Identity documents in the application.** Indian candidates: Aadhaar-UID (preferable), Passport, PAN Card, Voter ID, and Driving License. Foreign nationals: a valid passport, any other valid photo ID issued by their government, or a driving license. Upload the part of the photo ID that shows the photo, name, date of birth, and ID number. If the details are on two pages, upload both in a single PDF.

**EWS.** The application fee is the same as for General candidates. The qualifying mark is 90% of the General qualifying mark. GATE does not handle a later failure to produce an EWS certificate at admission or counselling. The EWS certificate is not uploaded during the application.

**Withdrawal.** The application fee is not refunded.

**Wrong paper, name spelling, or a new name after marriage.** Immediately after completion, the form cannot be changed. A later correction window exists. A paper change in that window requires an additional fee. A name correction requires supporting documents. A new name after marriage can be supported by uploading the photo ID with the old name together with an affidavit; the affidavit and photo ID should also be carried to the examination hall. Otherwise the application is provisional or rejected.

## Examination

**Which paper to choose.** GATE does not recommend a paper. The choice is the candidate’s.

**Using one paper’s score for a different programme.** Admission depends on the admitting institute’s own criteria.

**Preparation guidance.** GATE does not provide preparation guidance or coaching. The FAQ says aspirants may refer to previous years’ question papers and the official syllabus.

**Name mismatch between ID cards and educational documents.** GATE verifies identity from the photo ID submitted in the application. Academic documents are not used for identity verification during the examination. Admission criteria belong to the admitting institute.

**How many papers.** One or two, from the combinations on the Two-Paper Combinations page, out of the 30 papers. The candidate is expected to choose a paper appropriate to the qualifying degree, and may choose another paper according to an admission plan, keeping the admitting institute’s eligibility in mind.

**Multiple sessions.** The candidate is assigned only one session. It is printed on the admit card.

**Change of paper, city, or category after submission.** Only after the last date for application submission, and against additional payment. Dates will be announced later.

**Rough work and calculator.** One scribble pad at a time. The first pad must be returned before a second is issued. Pads are returned at the end. A virtual scientific calculator is on the screen. A physical calculator is not allowed.

**Entry and exit.** Login is not permitted later than 15 minutes after the start. Delayed entry does not add time. Candidates may leave only after the scheduled end.

**Items not permitted.** Mobile phones, physical calculators, wallets, watches of any type, electronic or communicating devices (including internet, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth devices such as smartwatches and smart glasses), books, papers, loose sheets, pen or pencil boxes or pouches, and similar items. There is no safekeeping arrangement. Possession of a physical calculator or a mobile phone, including a switched-off phone or a calculator on a watch, violates the code of conduct and leads to disqualification.

**Separate scores.** AR: no separate scores or ranks for Part B1 (Architecture) and Part B2 (Planning). GE: no separate scores or ranks for Surveying and Mapping and for Image Processing and Analysis. GG: separate scores and ranks are provided for Geology and Geophysics, using GA + Part A + the relevant Part B section. RA: no separate scores or ranks for Part B1 (Electrical) and Part B2 (Mechanical).

## PwD, dyslexia, scribe, and compensatory time

GATE 2027 follows O.M. F. No. P-13013/75/2023-Policy-DD-III dated 1 August 2025.

A scribe is available, on a valid disability certificate or UDID card, for blindness, locomotor disability (both arms only), and cerebral palsy. For other specified disabilities, a scribe requires a certificate that the person has a functional limitation to write and that a scribe is essential. That certificate is issued after medical examination by a multi-member medical authority chaired by a Chief Medical Officer, Civil Surgeon, Chief District Medical Officer, or Medical Superintendent of a government healthcare institution, using Appendix-B1.

A magnified question paper is available for visually challenged PwD candidates. Contact the GATE institute representative through the invigilator before the examination begins.

Dyslexic candidates can use a scribe and/or compensatory time. They submit a dyslexic certificate from the competent authorities named in the Information Brochure, and the functional-limitation certificate in the Appendix-B1 form. The scribe is provided by GATE 2027. Compensatory (extra) time of one hour applies to such dyslexic candidates who avail the scribe.

PwD candidates, including candidates with dyslexia or a similar learning disability, who have a functional limitation in writing may request compensatory time during the application even if they do not use a scribe. On-the-spot requests for a scribe or for compensatory time are not entertained.

If an eligible candidate uses a scribe, GATE 2027 provides the scribe. The scribe may only read the instructions and the test paper verbatim and assist with mouse clicks if the candidate cannot do so. The scribe must not translate, interpret, or emphasize the contents. Candidates with a disability in vision may view the paper in a magnified font. Scribe qualification follows the same office memorandum.

If it is discovered that a candidate used a scribe without the extent of disability that warrants a scribe, the GATE 2027 candidature stands disqualified.

## Admit card

The admit card is downloaded only from the GATE 2027 website. It is not sent by post or as an email attachment. The Important Dates page lists the download date as TBA.

The admit card alone is not enough. The candidate must also bring the original, valid photo identity document specified in the application. A photocopy, a scanned copy, or an expired document is not accepted. A soft copy of the admit card on a phone, tablet, or laptop is not accepted. A hard copy, preferably printed in colour, is required.

Indian identity cards accepted at entry: Aadhaar-UID (preferable), Passport, PAN card, Voter ID, and Driving License. Foreign nationals: a valid passport, any other valid photo ID issued by their government, or a driving license.

## Score card

- Marks displayed on GOAPS are for information only. An electronic or paper copy of that display is not valid.
- There is no hard-copy score card. Download it from GOAPS. During the download period at the end of March there is no fee.
- After that period, the score card can be downloaded from GOAPS by paying ₹500, up to 31 December 2027. The FAQ recommends keeping an electronic version.
- The score card is issued only to candidates who secured marks greater than or equal to the qualifying marks mentioned for the SC/ST/PwD category of that paper.
- The score card is valid for three years. The validity is also printed on the score card. Brochure section 8.2 states that validity runs for three years from the date of announcement of the results, and that scorecards will not be issued from 1 January 2028 onward.
- Net total marks may be negative for some candidates.
- A GATE score may be negative even when marks are positive, because of the score equation in the Information Brochure.
- Issued score-card data cannot be changed. A data change/correction window will be open during October 2026. Discrepancies after issue are taken up with the admitting institute, COAP, or the PSU.
- Candidates below the SC/ST/PwD qualifying marks of that paper can download a performance report at a later date, which will be announced on the website.

The score formula and the qualifying-marks formula are recorded from the Information Brochure in `opportunities.md`.
