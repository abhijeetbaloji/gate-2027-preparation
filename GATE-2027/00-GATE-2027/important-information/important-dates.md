## Dates the official sources leave as “to be announced”

- Admit card download: TBA on the Important Dates page. The FAQ says the admit card can only be downloaded from the GATE 2027 website and will not be sent by post or as an email attachment. The download date itself is not published.
- FAQ answers on changing the examination paper, examination city, or category after the last date for application submission say that the dates for those requests will be announced later.
- Brochure section 8: dates for displaying responses, answer keys, answer-key contests, final evaluation, and scorecard download will be announced on the official website after the examination.
- FAQ: candidates who obtain marks below the qualifying marks mentioned for the SC/ST/PwD category of that paper will be able to download a performance report at a later date. That date will be announced on the website.
