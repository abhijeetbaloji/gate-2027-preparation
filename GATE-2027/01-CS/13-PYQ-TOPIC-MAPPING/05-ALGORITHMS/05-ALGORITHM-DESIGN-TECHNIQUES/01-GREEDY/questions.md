### Q.55

**Paper:** GATE 2023 CS

**Question:**

Let G be a simple, finite, undirected graph with vertex set {v_{1}, . . . , v_{n}}. Let Δ(G)
denote the maximum degree of G and let N = {1, 2, . . .} denote the set of all possible
colors. Color the vertices of G using the following greedy strategy:
for i = 1, . . . , n
color(v_{i}) ← min{j ∈ N : no neighbour of v_{i} is colored j}
Which of the following statements is/are TRUE?

**Options:**

A. This procedure results in a proper vertex coloring of G.
B. The number of colors used is at most Δ(G) + 1.
C. The number of colors used is at most Δ(G).
D. The number of colors used is equal to the chromatic number of G.
