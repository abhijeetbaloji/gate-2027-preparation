## Notes

- **2026 CS-1, forenoon Q.55.** The stem is 2-colourability. Two options also state running times of a 2-colourability test.
- **2024 CS2 Q.60.** The graph is printed in the PDF.
- **2022 — Q.50.** One option is the chromatic number of the Petersen graph. Other options include a Hamiltonian path and isomorphism.
- **2020 — Q.52.** Minimum colours for an edge-colouring. The syllabus names colouring and does not separate vertex-colouring from edge-colouring.
- **2018 — Q.18.** The graph is printed in the PDF.
- **2016 CS-2 Q.3.** Minimum number of colours sufficient to vertex-colour any planar graph.
