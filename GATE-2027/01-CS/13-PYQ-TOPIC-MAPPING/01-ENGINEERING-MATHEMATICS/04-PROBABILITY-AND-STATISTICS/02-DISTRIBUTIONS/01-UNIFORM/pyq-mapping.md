## Notes

- **2025 CS-2 Q.64.** The asked quantity is the probability of a uniformly chosen square-invariant quadratic. The sample space is defined by a complex-polynomial condition, which is not a named Engineering Mathematics topic.
- **2024 CS1 Q.14.** A permutation is sampled uniformly. The options test independence of two events.
- **2020 — Q.17.** Probability that a random binary relation is reflexive.
- **2010 — Q.27.** Read from the PDF: the probability that a positive divisor of 10^99 is a multiple of 10^96. The sample space is the set of positive divisors.
