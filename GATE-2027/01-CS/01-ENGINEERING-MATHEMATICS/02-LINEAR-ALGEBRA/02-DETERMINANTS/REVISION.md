# Determinants — Quick Revision

- 2×2: ad − bc.
- Triangular det = product of diagonal.
- Row swap → sign flip; proportional rows → det 0.
- det(AB) = det(A)det(B); det(kA) = kⁿ det(A).
- det(A) = 0 ⇔ singular ⇔ non-invertible.
- Expand along sparsest row/column.
- Σλᵢ = tr(A); Πλᵢ = det(A).
- det(A + B) ≠ det(A) + det(B) generally.
