## Priority bands

### P1 — High Priority (≥16 mapped rows, or ≥13 with recent papers)

Propositional and First-Order Logic, Groups, Combinatorics (counting & recurrence), Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors, Continuity and Differentiability, Integration, Uniform Distribution, Conditional Probability.

### P2 — Medium Priority (6–15 mapped rows)

Relations, Functions, Graph connectivity & colouring, Determinants, Linear systems, Limits, Random variables, Mean.

### P3 — Lower Priority (0–5 mapped rows, or syllabus-only)

Sets, Partial orders, Monoids, Graph matching, Generating functions, Matrices, LU decomposition, Maxima/minima, MVT, Normal/Exponential/Poisson/Binomial, Median, Mode, Standard deviation, Bayes theorem.
