# Continuity and Differentiability — Quick Revision

- **Continuous at a:** f(a) defined, limit exists, limit = f(a).
- **f'(a) = lim_{h→0} [f(a+h)−f(a)]/h**
- **Differentiable at a ⇒ continuous at a** (not converse).
- **|x|:** continuous everywhere; diff except x = 0.
- **Rules:** product, quotient, chain; log diff for x^x.
- **Piecewise at c:** value match + derivative match.
- **MVT needs:** continuous [a,b], differentiable (a,b).
