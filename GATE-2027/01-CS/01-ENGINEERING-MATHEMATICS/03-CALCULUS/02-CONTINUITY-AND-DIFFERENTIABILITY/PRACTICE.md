## Answers and Explanations

**1.** f(a) defined; limit exists; limit=f(a).

**2.** **Yes**.

**3.** **No**.

**4.** **cos x**.

**5.** **Removable**.

**6.** **No** — undefined at 0.

**7.** **2xe^x + x²e^x**.

**8.** **−x/y**.

**9.** **Yes** (both sides 0).

**10.** **No** — left deriv 0, right 1.

**11.** Continuity: a+b=1; deriv: a=2 → **a=2, b=−1**.

**12.** **cos x / sin x = cot x** (where sin x>0).

**13.** At **x=±1** (corners of |u| at u=0).

**14.** f(x)−f(a)=((f(x)−f(a))/(x−a))(x−a)→f'(a)·0=0.

**15.** **No** — |x| on any interval containing 0.

**16.** **Yes** (standard theorem).

**17.** **Yes** — e.g. x² sin(1/x) type constructions; advanced.

**18.** **No** — not diff at 0.

**19.** Continuous; f'(x)=(2/3)x^{−1/3} → **not differentiable at 0** (infinite slope).

**20.** **|x|** — non-differentiable only at **x=0**.
