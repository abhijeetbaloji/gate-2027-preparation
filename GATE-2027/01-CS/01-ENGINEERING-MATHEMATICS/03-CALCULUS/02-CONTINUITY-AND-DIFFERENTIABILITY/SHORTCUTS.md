# Continuity and Differentiability — Shortcuts

## 1. |x| at 0

- **Why it works**: Corner: left slope −1, right +1
- **When to use**: Differentiability of |x|, |x−a|
- **Trap**: Still continuous at 0

## 2. Piecewise: 2 equations

- **Why it works**: Value + derivative at junction
- **When to use**: Find a, b parameters
- **Trap**: Need both for differentiability

## 3. Log diff shortcut

- **Why it works**: Turn products/powers into sums
- **When to use**: x^x, f^g
- **Trap**: Domain x > 0 for ln

## 4. Differentiable ⇒ continuous

- **Why it works**: One-way implication only
- **When to use**: Eliminate wrong MCQ options
- **Trap**: Don't reverse

## 5. Sum of |x−a_i|

- **Why it works**: Non-diff at each a_i
- **When to use**: Nested absolute values
- **Trap**: Count corners carefully
