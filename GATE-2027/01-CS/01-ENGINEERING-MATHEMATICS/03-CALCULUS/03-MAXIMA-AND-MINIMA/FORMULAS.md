# Maxima and Minima — Formulas

Reference only. Learn concepts in `NOTES.md` first.

| Formula | Meaning | Variables | Conditions | Derivation | Typical GATE use | Common mistake |
|---------|---------|-----------|------------|------------|------------------|----------------|
| f'(c)=0 | Critical point candidate | Interior c | f differentiable | Fermat (local int) | Find extrema | Assuming always extremum |
| f''>0 at c | Local minimum | f'(c)=0 | f'' exists | Taylor sign | Second deriv test | Using when f'≠0 |
| f''<0 at c | Local maximum | f'(c)=0 | f'' exists | Taylor sign | Second deriv test | f''=0 stop |
| EVT on [a,b] | Abs max/min exist | f continuous | Closed bounded | Weierstrass | Existence | Skipping endpoints |
| Compare f at {crit, a, b} | Absolute extrema | [a,b] | f continuous | EVT procedure | Interval max/min | Interior only |
| f'>0 | Increasing | on interval | — | MVT corollary | Monotonicity | Pointwise f'>0 |
| f''>0 | Concave up | on interval | — | Second deriv | Shape | Confuse with increasing |
