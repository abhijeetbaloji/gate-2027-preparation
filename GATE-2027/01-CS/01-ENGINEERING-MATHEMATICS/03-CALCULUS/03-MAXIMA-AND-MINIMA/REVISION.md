# Maxima and Minima — Quick Revision

- **Critical point:** f'(c)=0 or f' undefined.
- **1st deriv test:** +→− max; −→+ min.
- **2nd deriv test:** f'(c)=0, f''>0 min; f''<0 max; f''=0 inconclusive.
- **Closed [a,b]:** compare f at critical points + endpoints.
- **Applied:** express Q(x), Q'=0, check domain.
- **Trap:** x³ at 0 — critical but not extremum.
