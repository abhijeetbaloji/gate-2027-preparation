# Maxima and Minima — GATE-STYLE PRACTICE

## Level 1

**1.** Critical points of f(x) = x³ − 3x?

**2.** f'(2) = 0 and f''(2) > 0. Local min or max?

**3.** Minimum of x² on [−1, 2]?

### Level 1 — Solutions

1. **x = ±1**.
2. **Local minimum**.
3. **0** at x = 0.

## Level 2

**1.** Local extrema of f(x) = x⁴ − 4x²?

**2.** Rectangle perimeter 24 — dimensions for max area?

**3.** f(x) = x³ at x = 0: extremum?

### Level 2 — Solutions

1. **Max at 0 (f=0); min at ±√2 (f=−4)**.
2. **Square 6×6**, area 36.
3. **No** — inflection, f' does not change sign.

## Level 3

**1.** Absolute max of f(x) = x³ − 3x on [0, 2]?

**2.** Minimize f(x) = x² + 16/x for x > 0.

**3.** f(x) = sin x on [0, π]. Max and min?

### Level 3 — Solutions

1. Compare f(0)=0, f(1)=−2, f(2)=2 ⇒ **max 2 at x=2**.
2. f' = 2x − 16/x² = 0 ⇒ x³=8 ⇒ x=2, min **12**.
3. **Max 1 at π/2; min 0 at endpoints**.

## Level 4

**1.** Open box from square sheet — classic GATE setup (sketch method).

**2.** Show x³ − 3x has no global max on ℝ.

**3.** f''(c) = 0 at critical point — what next?

### Level 4 — Solutions

1. Cut squares of side x/4 from corners; optimize volume V(x).
2. f(x) → +∞ as x → +∞.
3. **First derivative test** or higher derivatives.

## Level 5 — Challenge

**1.** Find the point on y = x² closest to (0, 3) (minimize distance squared).

**2.** f(x) = x ln x on (0, ∞). Global minimum?

**3.** Prove f(x) = x⁴ − 4x³ + 6x² has no local maximum on ℝ.

### Level 5 — Solutions

1. Minimize D² = x² + (x²−3)². f'(x)=0 gives x satisfying 2x + 2x(x²−3)(2x)=0 → critical x=±√(3/2) type setup; minimum distance at **x = ±√(3/2)** (verify f'').
2. f'(x)=ln x + 1 = 0 ⇒ x = 1/e; f''>0 ⇒ **global min f(1/e) = −1/e**.
3. f'(x)=4x³−12x²+12x = 4x(x²−3x+3); discriminant of x²−3x+3 < 0 ⇒ only critical x=0; f''(0)=12>0 ⇒ **minimum at 0, no local max**.
