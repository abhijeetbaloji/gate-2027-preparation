# Maxima and Minima — Shortcuts

## 1. Quadratic vertex x = −b/(2a)

- **Why it works**: Parabola ax²+bx+c extremum at vertex
- **When to use**: Degree-2 only
- **Trap**: Check if max or min by sign of a

## 2. Endpoints on [a,b]

- **Why it works**: Continuous f always attains max/min on closed interval
- **When to use**: Absolute extrema
- **Trap**: Don't skip a and b

## 3. Minimize d² not d

- **Why it works**: Same minimizer, easier derivative
- **When to use**: Distance problems
- **Trap**: Take sqrt only at end if needed

## 4. f'' quick test

- **Why it works**: One evaluation at critical point
- **When to use**: When f'(c)=0 and f'' easy
- **Trap**: Inconclusive if f''=0

## 5. Fixed perimeter square

- **Why it works**: Rectangle max area is square
- **When to use**: Classic applied template
- **Trap**: Verify perimeter constraint
