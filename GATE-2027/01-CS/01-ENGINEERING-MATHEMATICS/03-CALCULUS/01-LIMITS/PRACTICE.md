**5.** Is 0/0 always equal to 0?

## Level 2 — Standard GATE Style

## Level 4 — Trap Questions

**15.** lim_{x→0} (x² sin(1/x))?

**16.** Can L'Hôpital apply to lim (x−sin x)/(x³) at 0?

**17.** lim_{x→∞} (x+sin x)/(x−cos x)?

**18.** lim_{x→0} (1/x) exists?

---

## Level 5 — Challenge

**19.** Prove lim_{x→0} (1+x)^{1/x}=e using ln.

**20.** Find all a such that lim_{x→1} (x²−ax+1)/(x−1) exists (finite).

---

## Answers and Explanations

**1.** **7**.

**2.** **1**.

**3.** Left ≠ right, or oscillation, or unbounded.

**4.** **0**.

**5.** **No** — indeterminate.

**6.** **4**.

**7.** **1/2** (L'Hôpital or 1−cos=2sin²(x/2)).

**8.** **3**.

**9.** **2**.

**10.** Left −1, right 1 → **DNE**.

**11.** **3/5**.

**12.** **−1/2**.

**13.** Multiply conjugate → **0**.

**14.** e^{x ln x}→e⁰=**1**.

**15.** Squeeze: |x² sin(1/x)|≤x² → **0**.

**16.** **Yes** — 0/0; gives 1/6 after repeated use.

**17.** **1** (divide by x).

**18.** **No** — unbounded.

**19.** Let L=lim; ln L = lim ln(1+x)/x = 1 → L=e.

**20.** Need cancel (x−1): x²−ax+1=0 at x=1 → 2−a=0 → **a=2**; then limit = 2−2=0.
