# Limits — Shortcuts

## 1. sin(ax)/x → a

- **Why it works**: Standard limit with scaling
- **When to use**: Any sin(ax)/x as x→0
- **Trap**: Must be x→0, not ∞

## 2. Leading coefficient ratio

- **Why it works**: Highest-degree terms dominate at ∞
- **When to use**: Polynomial rational limits at ∞
- **Trap**: Only when degrees equal

## 3. Factor before L'Hôpital

- **Why it works**: Algebra often faster than two derivatives
- **When to use**: Rational 0/0
- **Trap**: Don't skip when factorization is obvious

## 4. e limit rewrite

- **Why it works**: (1+kx)^{1/x} = [(1+kx)^{1/(kx)}]^k → e^k
- **When to use**: Exponential limit forms
- **Trap**: Identify k correctly

## 5. Conjugate rationalize

- **Why it works**: Eliminates √( ) − √( ) forms
- **When to use**: √ limits at 0
- **Trap**: Multiply numerator and denominator
