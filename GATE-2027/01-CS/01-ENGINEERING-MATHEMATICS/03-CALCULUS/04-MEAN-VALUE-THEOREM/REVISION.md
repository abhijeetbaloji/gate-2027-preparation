# Mean Value Theorem — Quick Revision

- **Rolle:** f cont [a,b], diff (a,b), f(a)=f(b) ⇒ ∃c∈(a,b): f'(c)=0.
- **Lagrange MVT:** same hypotheses ⇒ ∃c: f'(c)=(f(b)−f(a))/(b−a).
- **c is in open interval (a,b)**.
- **Cont on closed, diff on open** — both required.
- **Use:** existence of derivative value; bound |f(b)−f(a)| ≤ M|b−a|.
