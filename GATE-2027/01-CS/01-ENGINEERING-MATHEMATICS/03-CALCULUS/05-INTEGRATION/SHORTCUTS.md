# Integration — Shortcuts

## 1. LIATE for parts

- **Why it works**: Choose u in order Log, Inverse, Algebraic, Trig, Exp
- **When to use**: Integration by parts
- **Trap**: Wrong u makes integral harder

## 2. Definite sub: change limits

- **Why it works**: Avoid back-substituting x
- **When to use**: u-substitution on [a,b]
- **Trap**: Must update bounds

## 3. Even function on [−a,a]

- **Why it works**: Integral = 2∫_0^a f
- **When to use**: Symmetric integrands
- **Trap**: f must be even

## 4. ∫ln x dx = x ln x − x

- **Why it works**: Memorize parts result
- **When to use**: Log integrals
- **Trap**: Add +C if indefinite

## 5. Triangle area check

- **Why it works**: Verify ∫ linear f by geometry
- **When to use**: Quick sanity check
- **Trap**: Only for simple regions
