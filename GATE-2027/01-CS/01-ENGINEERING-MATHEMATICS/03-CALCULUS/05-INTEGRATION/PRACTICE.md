# Integration — GATE-STYLE PRACTICE

## Level 1

**1.** ∫₀¹ x² dx?

**2.** ∫ x e^x dx?

**3.** ∫₀^π sin x dx?

### Level 1 — Solutions

1. **1/3**.
2. **e^x(x−1) + C**.
3. **2**.

## Level 2

**1.** ∫ 1/(1+x²) dx?

**2.** ∫₀^2 x√(4−x²) dx (substitution)?

**3.** Average of f(x)=x on [0,4]?

### Level 2 — Solutions

1. **tan⁻¹x + C**.
2. u=4−x² ⇒ **8/3**.
3. **2**.

## Level 3

**1.** ∫ x² ln x dx?

**2.** ∫ 1/(x²−1) dx?

**3.** Area under y=x from 0 to 3?

### Level 3 — Solutions

1. **x³/9 · ln x − x³/9 + C** (parts).
2. **(1/2)ln|(x−1)/(x+1)| + C**.
3. **9/2**.

## Level 4

**1.** ∫ e^x sin x dx (parts twice).

**2.** ∫₀^1 x(1−x)^n dx (Beta-type).

### Level 4 — Solutions

1. **e^x(sin x − cos x)/2 + C**.
2. **1/((n+1)(n+2))** via substitution u=1−x.

## Level 5 — Challenge

**1.** ∫₀^∞ e^{−x²} dx = √π/2 (recognition — state value, no full proof required).

**2.** ∫ ln(sin x) dx from 0 to π/2 (GATE may test setup; answer involves −(π/2)ln 2).

**3.** Show ∫₀^a f(x) dx + ∫₀^b f(a+b−x) dx = ∫₀^{a+b} f(x) dx when f integrable (substitution u=a+b−x).

### Level 5 — Solutions

1. **√π/2** — Gaussian integral (standard result).
2. Use symmetry/substitution; classic result **−(π/2)ln 2**.
3. Substitute u = a+b−x in first integral; limits swap to give second half of [0,a+b].
