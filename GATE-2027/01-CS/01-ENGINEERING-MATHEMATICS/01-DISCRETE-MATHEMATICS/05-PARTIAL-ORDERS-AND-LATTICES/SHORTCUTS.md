# Partial Orders and Lattices — Shortcuts

Only use shortcuts **after** you understand `NOTES.md`.

---

### Shortcut: Divisors poset extrema


**Why it works:** Definition


**When to use:** On {1,…,n}| divides


**Example:** 1 minimal bottom; primes incomparable


**Trap / limitation:** Reverse divisibility direction

---

### Shortcut: Power set lattice


**Why it works:** Set operations


**When to use:** Subset order


**Example:** ∪ and ∩


**Trap / limitation:** ⊂ vs ⊆ on Hasse

---

### Shortcut: Find cover edges


**Why it works:** Remove transitive


**When to use:** Build Hasse


**Example:** 1→2→6 keep 1→2,2→6 not 1→6


**Trap / limitation:** Keep transitive edge
