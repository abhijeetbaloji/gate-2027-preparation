# Partial Orders and Lattices — Mistakes

## Common Mistakes

These are **general/common traps** — not personal error logs.


---

### General/common trap: Maximal vs maximum


**What goes wrong:** Assuming unique maximal


**Correct rule:** Maximum must beat all


**Prevention:** Check comparability to every element


---

### General/common trap: Not a lattice


**What goes wrong:** Pair lacks LUB in subset


**Correct rule:** Both join and meet in set


**Prevention:** Test incomparable pairs
