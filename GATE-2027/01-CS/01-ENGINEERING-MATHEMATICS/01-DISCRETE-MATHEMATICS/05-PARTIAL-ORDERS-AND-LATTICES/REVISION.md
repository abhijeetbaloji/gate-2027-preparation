# Partial Orders and Lattices — Revision Sheet

Use **after** studying `NOTES.md`.

- PO: reflexive + antisymmetric + transitive.
- Hasse: cover relations only, smaller below.
- Minimal/maximal vs least/greatest — uniqueness differs.
- Lattice: every pair has LUB (∨) and GLB (∧).
- Divisibility poset: 1 bottom, join=LCM, meet=GCD when in set.
- P(S),⊆: ∪=join, ∩=meet.
- a≤b ⇔ a∧b=a ⇔ a∨b=b.
