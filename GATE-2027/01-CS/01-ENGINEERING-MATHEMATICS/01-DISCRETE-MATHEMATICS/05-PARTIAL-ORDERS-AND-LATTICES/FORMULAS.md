# Partial Orders and Lattices — Formulas

Reference only. Learn concepts in `NOTES.md` first.


| Formula | Meaning | Variables | Conditions | Derivation | Typical GATE use | Common mistake |
|---------|---------|-----------|------------|------------|------------------|----------------|
| a∨b | Join / LUB | a,b∈L | Lattice | Least upper bound | Compute in poset | Confuse with max element |
| a∧b | Meet / GLB | a,b∈L | Lattice | Greatest lower bound | Compute | Confuse with min |
| n(n+1)/2 | Comparable pairs in chain n | n | Finite chain | Count pairs | Chain length | Off-by-one |
| ⊥, ⊤ | Bottom / top | — | If exist unique | Bounds | Power set | Assume always exist |
