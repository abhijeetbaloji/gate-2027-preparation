## 1. Groups in GATE

## Monoid plus inverses

### Concept

**Group (G,·):** closed associative binary op, identity e, each a has inverse a⁻¹ with a·a⁻¹=e.

### Intuition

Every action can be undone.

### GATE Connection

Verify axioms; subgroups; Lagrange; cyclic groups; S_n.

### Traps

(ℚ,×) not a group — 0 lacks inverse.


## 2. Uniqueness lemmas

## Identity and inverses

### Concept

Identity is unique; inverse of each element is unique.

### Derivation

Standard uniqueness proofs via assuming two identities/inverses.

### GATE Connection

Conceptual MCQs.

### Traps

Assuming operation commutative — only Abelian groups.


## 3. Abelian groups

## Commutative groups

### Concept

Group with a·b=b·a for all a,b.

### Examples

(ℤ,+), (ℤ_n,+), (ℝ*,×) nonzero reals.

### GATE Connection

Notation additive vs multiplicative.

### Traps

Matrix groups usually non-Abelian.


## 4. Lagrange's theorem

## Subgroup size divides group size

### Concept

If H≤G finite, |H| divides |G|. Index [G:H]=|G|/|H|.

### Intuition

Cosets partition G into equal-sized blocks.

### Derivation

Left cosets of H partition G, each of size |H|.

### Examples

|G|=12 ⇒ subgroup orders ∈ {1,2,3,4,6,12}.

### GATE Connection

Eliminate impossible subgroup sizes.

### Traps

Converse false — divisors may not occur as subgroup orders.


## 5. Element order

## Cyclic powers

### Concept

Order of a is smallest k>0 with a^k=e; in finite group, order divides |G|.

### Examples

In ℤ_6, order of 2 is 3 because 2+2+2=0.

### GATE Connection

Compute orders in ℤ_n under addition.

### Traps

Using |G| as order of every element — only for cyclic of prime order.


## 6. Cyclic groups

## Generated by one element

### Concept

G=<g>={e,g,g²,…}. Finite cyclic order n isomorphic to ℤ_n.

### Examples

ℤ_7 = <1> under addition mod 7.

### GATE Connection

Count generators via φ(n).

### Traps

Every group of prime order is cyclic — powerful shortcut.


## 7. Permutation group S_n

## Symmetric group

### Concept

S_n = all bijections on n points; |S_n|=n!.

### Examples

|S_3|=6; composition is non-commutative.

### GATE Connection

Count permutations; compose cycles (basic).

### Traps

Confusing |S_n| with 2^n.


## 8. Subgroups of ℤ_n

## Divisor correspondence

### Concept

Subgroups of ℤ_n under + correspond to divisors of n.

### Examples

Subgroups of ℤ_12: sizes 1,2,3,4,6,12.

### GATE Connection

List all subgroups by divisor lattice.

### Traps

Missing the whole group as subgroup.


## 9. Inverses and products

## Algebraic identities

### Concept

(ab)⁻¹=b⁻¹a⁻¹; (a⁻¹)⁻¹=a.

### Derivation

Multiply ab by b⁻¹a⁻¹.

### GATE Connection

Simplify expressions in group MCQs.

### Traps

Reversing order in non-Abelian group.


## 10. Modular groups

## ℤ_n and units

### Concept

(ℤ_n,+) always group. Multiplicative group of units (ℤ_n*,×) for coprime residues.

### Examples

ℤ_8*: {1,3,5,7}.

### GATE Connection

Check closure under × mod n.

### Traps

All nonzero residues mod n need not be units.


## 11. Summary

## Workflow

### Concept

Verify group axioms → use Lagrange → classify cyclic/subgroup structure.

### GATE Connection

Prime |G| ⇒ cyclic.

### Traps

Subtracting as group operation on ℤ — use (ℤ,+) instead.
