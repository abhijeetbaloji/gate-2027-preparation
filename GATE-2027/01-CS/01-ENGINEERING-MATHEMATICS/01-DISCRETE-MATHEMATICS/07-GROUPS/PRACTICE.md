# Groups — GATE-STYLE PRACTICE

**These are practice questions only — not GATE PYQs.**

---

## Level 1 — Concept Check

**1.** State the four group axioms.

**2.** Is (ℤ, +) abelian?

**3.** In a group, (ab)⁻¹ = ?

**4.** |S₄| = ?

**5.** Is every group of prime order cyclic?

---

## Level 2 — Standard GATE Style

**6.** Is (ℚ, ×) a group?

**7.** Order of element 3 in (ℤ₇, +)?

**8.** List all subgroups of (ℤ₆, +).

**9.** How many generators does ℤ₉ have?

**10.** Verify (ℤ₅*, ×) is a group. What is its order?

---

## Level 3 — Multi-Step

**11.** |G| = 15. What are possible orders of elements?

**12.** Find order of 2 in ℤ₁₁* (multiplicative mod 11).

**13.** Is S₃ abelian? Give a counterexample if not.

**14.** H = {0,4,8} in ℤ₁₂. Is H a subgroup? List cosets.

**15.** Prove: in finite group, a^|G| = e.

---

## Level 4 — Trap Questions

**16.** Lagrange: if d | |G|, subgroup of order d always exists. True?

**17.** (ℤ₁₀, +) — how many elements have order 10?

**18.** Identity in a group is unique. Inverse of a is unique. Both true?

**19.** |ℤ₁₂| = φ(12). True or false?

**20.** Klein four group is cyclic. True or false?

---

## Level 5 — Challenge

**21.** Show every subgroup of cyclic group is cyclic.

**22.** Count elements of order 3 in ℤ₉.

**23.** If |G|=p prime, prove center Z(G)=G.

**24.** Write multiplication table for ℤ₃ and identify generator.

**25.** φ(12)=4 — list generators of (ℤ₁₂, +).

---

## Answers and Explanations

**1.** Closure, associativity, identity, inverses.

**2.** **Yes** — a+b=b+a.

**3.** **b⁻¹a⁻¹**.

**4.** 4! = **24**.

**5.** **Yes** (Lagrange + no proper subgroups).

**6.** **No** — 0 has no inverse.

**7.** 7/gcd(3,7)=**7** (add 3 seven times mod 7 to get 0).

**8.** {0}, {0,3}, {0,2,4}, {0,1,2,3,4,5} — orders 1,2,3,6.

**9.** φ(9)=**6** generators (coprime to 9: 1,2,4,5,7,8).

**10.** Units {1,2,3,4}; closed; inverses exist mod 5 → group of order **4**.

**11.** Element orders divide 15 → **1, 3, 5, or 15**.

**12.** Powers of 2 mod 11: 2,4,8,5,10,9,7,3,6,1 → order **10**.

**13.** **No** — (1 2)(1 3) ≠ (1 3)(1 2) in S₃.

**14.** **Yes** — closed, inverses in H; cosets: H and 1+H={1,5,9}.

**15.** ⟨a⟩ has size dividing |G|; a^|⟨a⟩|=e ⇒ a^|G|=e.

**16.** **False** in general (A₄ counterexample); true for cyclic ℤ_n.

**17.** Generators coprime to 10: **1,3,7,9** → **4** elements of order 10.

**18.** **Both true**.

**19.** **False** — |ℤ₁₂|=12; φ(12)=4 (generators count).

**20.** **False** — no element of order 4; not cyclic.

**21.** Subgroup of ⟨g⟩ is ⟨g^d⟩ for some d — cyclic.

**22.** Elements a with 3a≡0 mod 9, a≠0: **3,6** → **2** elements.

**23.** Center has prime order dividing p; |Z|>1 ⇒ Z=G.

**24.** Table: 0+0=0, 1+1=2, 2+2=1, etc.; **1** (or 2) generates.

**25.** **1, 5, 7, 11** (gcd with 12 is 1).
