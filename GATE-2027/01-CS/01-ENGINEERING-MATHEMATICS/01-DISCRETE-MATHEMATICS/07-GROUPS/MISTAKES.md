# Groups — Mistakes

## Common Mistakes

These are **general/common traps** — not personal error logs.


---

### General/common trap: (ℚ,×) group


**What goes wrong:** Including 0


**Correct rule:** 0 has no inverse


**Prevention:** Exclude non-units


---

### General/common trap: Lagrange converse


**What goes wrong:** Every divisor is subgroup order


**Correct rule:** Only divides


**Prevention:** Check A_4 example awareness
