# Graphs: Matching — Quick Revision

- Matching: edges pairwise vertex-disjoint.
- Maximum vs maximal vs perfect (covers all V, |V| even).
- Bipartite: parts A, B; edges only A–B; no odd cycle.
- Hall: saturate A iff |N(S)| ≥ |S| for all S ⊆ A.
- König (bipartite): max matching = min vertex cover.
- K_{m,n}: max matching = min(m, n); perfect if m = n.
- P_n: max matching = ⌊n/2⌋; star: 1.
- K_n: n/2 if n even; (n−1)/2 if n odd.
- Perfect matchings in K_{n,n}: n! bijections.
