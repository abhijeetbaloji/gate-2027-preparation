# Sets — Mistakes Log

### Common Mistakes

1. **General/common trap:** Confusing ∈ (element) with ⊆ (subset). Example: 1 ∈ {1,2} but {1} ⊆ {1,2}.

2. **General/common trap:** Treating {∅} as empty — it is a one-element set containing ∅.

3. **General/common trap:** |P({∅})| = 2, not 1. Elements are ∅ and {∅}.

4. **General/common trap:** Forgetting universe U when taking complement — A^c depends on U.

5. **General/common trap:** Using |A∪B| = |A|+|B| without subtracting |A∩B|.

6. **General/common trap:** A − B ≠ B − A in general.

7. **General/common trap:** Cartesian product size is product, not sum: |A×B| = |A|·|B|.

8. **General/common trap:** "Subsets of n-set" = 2^n, not n or n!.

9. **General/common trap:** In three-set Venn problems, omitting |A∩B∩C| in inclusion–exclusion.

10. **General/common trap:** Assuming ∅ ∈ A for all A — false.
