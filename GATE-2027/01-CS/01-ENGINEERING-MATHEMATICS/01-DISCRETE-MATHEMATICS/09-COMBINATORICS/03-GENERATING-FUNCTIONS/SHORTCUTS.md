# Generating Functions — Shortcuts

Only use shortcuts **after** you understand `NOTES.md`.

---

### Shortcut: Stars and bars GF


**Why it works:** 1/(1−x)^k


**When to use:** Identical into boxes


**Example:** coefficient of x^r


**Trap / limitation:** k vs r swap

---

### Shortcut: Partial fractions


**Why it works:** Extract a_n


**When to use:** Rational G(x)


**Example:** A/(1−r₁x)+…


**Trap / limitation:** Algebra mistake
