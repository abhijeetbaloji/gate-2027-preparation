# Combinatorics: Counting — Shortcuts

Only use shortcuts **after** you understand `NOTES.md`.

---

### Shortcut: C(n,r)=C(n,n−r)


**Why it works:** Complement selection


**When to use:** Large r


**Example:** C(100,98)=C(100,2)


**Trap / limitation:** r>n

---

### Shortcut: At least one


**Why it works:** Complement counting


**When to use:** Easier than IEP


**Example:** 1−none


**Trap / limitation:** Overlaps

---

### Shortcut: Pigeonhole with remainders


**Why it works:** Mod classes


**When to use:** Same remainder


**Example:** n+1 integers mod n


**Trap / limitation:** Not enough pigeons
