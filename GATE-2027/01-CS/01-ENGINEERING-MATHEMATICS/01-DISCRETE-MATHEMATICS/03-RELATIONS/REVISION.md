# Relations — Revision Sheet

Use **after** studying `NOTES.md`.

## Properties (on A×A)

- **Reflexive:** all (a,a)
- **Symmetric:** reverse pairs
- **Antisymmetric:** no 2-cycle between distinct
- **Transitive:** a→b→c ⇒ a→c

## Special types

- **Equivalence:** R+S+T → partition
- **Partial order:** R+A+T
- **Total order:** PO + all comparable

## Counting (|A|=n)

- All relations: **2^(n²)**
- Reflexive: **2^(n²−n)**
- Symmetric: **2^(n(n+1)/2)**

## Closures

- Reflexive: + diagonal
- Symmetric: + reverses
- Transitive: + reachability

## Traps

- Empty on nonempty set: **not reflexive**
- Antisymmetric ≠ asymmetric
- Check **composition order**
