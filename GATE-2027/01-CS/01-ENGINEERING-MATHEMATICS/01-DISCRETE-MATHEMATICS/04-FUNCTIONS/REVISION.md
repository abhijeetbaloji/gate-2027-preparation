# Functions — Revision Sheet

Use **after** studying `NOTES.md`.

- f: A→B: exactly one output per input.
- Total maps: |B|^|A|; injections P(n,m); bijections m! when |A|=|B|.
- Onto: IEP formula; n=2 ⇒ 2^m−2.
- Finite self-map: 1-1 ⇔ onto ⇔ bijective.
- (g∘f)(a)=g(f(a)); inverse iff bijective.
- ⌊x⌋≤x<⌊x⌋+1; floor negatives carefully.
- Range ⊆ codomain; onto means range=codomain.
