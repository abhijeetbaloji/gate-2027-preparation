# Functions — Formulas

Reference only. Learn concepts in `NOTES.md` first.


| Formula | Meaning | Variables | Conditions | Derivation | Typical GATE use | Common mistake |
|---------|---------|-----------|------------|------------|------------------|----------------|
| n^m | All functions A→B | m=|A|, n=|B| | Finite sets | Product rule | Count maps | m^n vs n^m |
| n!/(n−m)! | Injections | m≤n | Finite | Permutation | 1-1 maps | m>n → 0 |
| m! | Bijections | m=n | Finite | Permutations | Bijections | n! vs m! |
| Σ(−1)^i C(n,i)(n−i)^m | Onto functions | m≥n | Finite | IEP | Surjections | Missing (−1)^i |
| (g∘f)(x) | Composition | — | Types match | Definition | Combine maps | Order swap |
| ⌊x⌋ | Floor | x∈ℝ | — | Greatest int ≤x | Bounds | Truncate toward 0 error |
