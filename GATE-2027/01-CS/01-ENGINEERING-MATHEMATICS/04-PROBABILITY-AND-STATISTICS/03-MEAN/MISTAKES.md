# Mean — Mistakes

## Common Mistakes

These are **general/common traps** — not personal error logs.

### General/common trap: Mean of means

**What goes wrong:** Averaging group means with unequal sizes

**Correct rule:** Weight by nᵢ

**Prevention:** Overall=(n₁x̄₁+n₂x̄₂)/(n₁+n₂)

---

### General/common trap: E[g(X)]

**What goes wrong:** Using g(E[X])

**Correct rule:** E[g(X)]≠g(E[X])

**Prevention:** Compute E[g(X)] properly

---

### General/common trap: Outliers

**What goes wrong:** Using mean for skewed data

**Correct rule:** Consider median

**Prevention:** Check data shape
