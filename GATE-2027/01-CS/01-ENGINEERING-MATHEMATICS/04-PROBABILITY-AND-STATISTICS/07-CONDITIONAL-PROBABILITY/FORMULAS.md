# Conditional Probability — Formulas

Reference only. Learn concepts in `NOTES.md` first.

| Formula | Meaning | Variables | Conditions | Derivation | Typical GATE use | Common mistake |
|---------|---------|-----------|------------|------------|------------------|----------------|
| Definition | P(A∩B)/P(B) | P(B)>0 | — | Restricted space | All conditional Qs | Swapping A,B |
| Multiplication | P(B)P(A|B) | — | — | Chain rule | Sequential events | Order confusion |
| Independence | P(A∩B)=P(A)P(B) | — | Equivalent forms | No influence | Coin flips | Without replacement |
| Total probability | ΣP(A|Bᵢ)P(Bᵢ) | Partition Bᵢ | Exhaustive | Mixture | Factories | Non-partition |
