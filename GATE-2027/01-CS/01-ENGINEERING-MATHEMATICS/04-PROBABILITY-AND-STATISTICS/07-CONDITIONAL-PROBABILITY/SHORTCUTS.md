# Conditional Probability — Shortcuts

## 1. Restrict sample space

- **Why it works**: List only outcomes consistent with condition
- **When to use**: Given at least one H, etc.
- **Trap**: Don't use full sample space

## 2. Tree diagram

- **Why it works**: Multiply along branches
- **When to use**: Multi-stage problems
- **Trap**: Label each branch probability

## 3. Total probability first

- **Why it works**: Compute P(A) before Bayes
- **When to use**: Mixture/factory problems
- **Trap**: Need partition of Ω

## 4. Without replacement: count cards

- **Why it works**: Hypergeometric thinking
- **When to use**: Card draws
- **Trap**: Probabilities change each draw

## 5. Independence check

- **Why it works**: P(A∩B)=P(A)P(B)?
- **When to use**: Verify before multiplying marginals
- **Trap**: Replacement matters
