# Conditional Probability — Quick Revision

- **P(A|B) = P(A∩B)/P(B)** (P(B)>0).
- **P(A∩B) = P(B)P(A|B)** — multiplication rule.
- **Independent:** P(A∩B)=P(A)P(B) or P(A|B)=P(A).
- **Total probability:** P(A)=Σ P(A|B_i)P(B_i) over partition.
- **Trap:** P(A|B)≠P(B|A); "≥1H" → 3 outcomes; no replacement ≠ independent.
