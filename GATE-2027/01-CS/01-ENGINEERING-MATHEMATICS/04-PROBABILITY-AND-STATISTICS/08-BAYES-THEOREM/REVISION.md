# Bayes' Theorem — Quick Revision

- **P(A|B) = P(B|A)P(A) / P(B)**.
- **P(B) = Σ P(B|A_i)P(A_i)** — total probability denominator.
- **Two-hypothesis:** P(H|D) = P(D|H)P(H) / [P(D|H)P(H)+P(D|¬H)P(¬H)].
- **Prior** = before evidence; **posterior** = after.
- **Trap:** P(A|B)≠P(B|A); rare disease ⇒ low P(disease|+).
