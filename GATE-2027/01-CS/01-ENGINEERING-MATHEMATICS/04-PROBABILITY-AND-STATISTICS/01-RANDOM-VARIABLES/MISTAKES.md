# Random Variables — Mistakes

## Common Mistakes

These are **general/common traps** — not personal error logs.

### General/common trap: PDF exceeds 1

**What goes wrong:** Rejecting valid PDF because f(x)>1

**Correct rule:** Only integral must equal 1

**Prevention:** Check ∫f=1 not max f

---

### General/common trap: Point probability continuous

**What goes wrong:** Computing P(X=3) for continuous X

**Correct rule:** P(X=x)=0; use intervals

**Prevention:** Use CDF difference

---

### General/common trap: E[X²] vs (E[X])²

**What goes wrong:** Confusing second moment with squared mean

**Correct rule:** Var=E[X²]−E[X]²

**Prevention:** Two different quantities

---

### General/common trap: Var of sum without independence

**What goes wrong:** Var(X+Y)=Var(X)+Var(Y) always

**Correct rule:** Need independence or Cov=0

**Prevention:** Check independence first
