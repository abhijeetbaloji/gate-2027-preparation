# Random Variables — Shortcuts

Only use shortcuts **after** you understand the underlying concepts in `NOTES.md`.

---

### Shortcut: Var from two moments

**Why it works:** E[X²]−E[X]² in one step

**When to use:** Given E[X] and E[X²]

**Example:** E[X]=3,E[X²]=13→Var=4

**Trap / limitation:** Don't re-expand definition

---

### Shortcut: Indicator variable

**Why it works:** E[I]=P(event) for I∈{0,1}

**When to use:** Counting problems

**Example:** P(ace)=E[I_ace]

**Trap / limitation:** I must be 0/1

---

### Shortcut: Uniform {1,…,n}

**Why it works:** E[X]=(n+1)/2 instantly

**When to use:** Discrete uniform

**Example:** Die: E=3.5

**Trap / limitation:** Not for continuous
