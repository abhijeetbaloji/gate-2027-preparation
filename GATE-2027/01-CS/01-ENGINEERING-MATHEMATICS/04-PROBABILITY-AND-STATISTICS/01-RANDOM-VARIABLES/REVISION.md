# Random Variables — Revision Sheet

Use **after** studying `NOTES.md`.

## Core formulas
- PMF: Σp(x)=1; PDF: ∫f(x)dx=1
- CDF: F(x)=P(X≤x); P(a<X≤b)=F(b)−F(a)
- E[X]=Σxp(x) or ∫xf(x)dx
- Var(X)=E[X²]−E[X]²; σ=√Var(X)
- E[aX+b]=aE[X]+b; Var(aX+b)=a²Var(X)

## Independence
- E[XY]=E[X]E[Y]; Var(X+Y)=Var(X)+Var(Y)

## Traps
- PDF can exceed 1
- P(X=x)=0 for continuous X
- E[g(X)]≠g(E[X])
