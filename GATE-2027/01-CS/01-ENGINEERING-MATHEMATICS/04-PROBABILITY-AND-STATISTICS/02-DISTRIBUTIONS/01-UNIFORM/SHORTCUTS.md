# Uniform Distribution — Shortcuts

Only use shortcuts **after** you understand the underlying concepts in `NOTES.md`.

---

### Shortcut: Interval probability

**Why it works:** Ratio of lengths, no integral

**When to use:** Continuous uniform

**Example:** P(X>6) on [2,8]=(8−6)/6

**Trap / limitation:** Only on [a,b]

---

### Shortcut: Midpoint mean

**Why it works:** (a+b)/2 for any uniform

**When to use:** Both discrete and continuous

**Example:** [3,7]→E=5

**Trap / limitation:** —

---

### Shortcut: Die expectation

**Why it works:** E=(n+1)/2 for {1,…,n}

**When to use:** Fair die n=6→3.5

**Example:** —

**Trap / limitation:** Not (1+6)/2 wrong n
