# Uniform Distribution — Formulas

Reference only. Learn concepts in `NOTES.md` first.

| Formula | Meaning | Variables | Conditions | Derivation | Typical GATE use | Common mistake |
|---------|---------|-----------|------------|------------|------------------|----------------|
| Discrete PMF | 1/n | n=b−a+1 | Equally likely | Symmetry | Die, random int | Using b−a for n |
| Continuous PDF | 1/(b−a) | x∈[a,b] | Area=1 | Flat density | Wait times | Outside [a,b]=0 |
| Mean | (a+b)/2 | Both types | Midpoint | Symmetry | Quick E[X] | — |
| Var continuous | (b−a)²/12 | — | — | Integration | Spread | Confusing with discrete formula |
| Interval prob | (d−c)/(b−a) | [c,d]⊆[a,b] | — | Proportion | No integration | Wrong bounds |
