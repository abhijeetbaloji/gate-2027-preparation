# Poisson Distribution — Shortcuts

Only use shortcuts **after** you understand the underlying concepts in `NOTES.md`.

---

### Shortcut: Mean=Var

**Why it works:** Poisson first when E=Var

**When to use:** Identification

**Example:** E=4,Var=4→Poi(4)

**Trap / limitation:** —

---

### Shortcut: P(X=0)

**Why it works:** e^{−λ}

**When to use:** Fastest single prob

**Example:** λ=3→e^{−3}

**Trap / limitation:** —

---

### Shortcut: At least one

**Why it works:** 1−e^{−λ}

**When to use:** Complement

**Example:** λ=2→1−e^{−2}

**Trap / limitation:** Don't sum series
