# Exponential Distribution — Mistakes

## Common Mistakes

These are **general/common traps** — not personal error logs.

### General/common trap: Rate vs mean

**What goes wrong:** Using λ as mean

**Correct rule:** Mean=1/λ

**Prevention:** Read whether rate or mean given

---

### General/common trap: Memoryless misuse

**What goes wrong:** Applying to non-exponential

**Correct rule:** Only Exp on [0,∞)

**Prevention:** Check distribution type

---

### General/common trap: Time units

**What goes wrong:** Mixing minutes and hours for λ

**Correct rule:** Scale λ with time unit

**Prevention:** Convert interval first
