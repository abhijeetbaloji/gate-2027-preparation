# Normal Distribution — Formulas

Reference only. Learn concepts in `NOTES.md` first.

| Formula | Meaning | Variables | Conditions | Derivation | Typical GATE use | Common mistake |
|---------|---------|-----------|------------|------------|------------------|----------------|
| Standardize | Z=(X−μ)/σ | σ>0 | X~N(μ,σ²) | Unit variance | All normal Qs | Using σ² as σ |
| Linear transform | aX+b ~ N(aμ+b,a²σ²) | — | — | Var scales a² | Transformed RV | Adding variances wrong |
| Sum independent | N(μ₁+μ₂,σ₁²+σ₂²) | Independent | — | MGF/additivity | Total error | Without independence |
| Tail symmetry | P(Z>z)=P(Z<−z) | — | — | Symmetry | Negative z | Sign errors |
