# Normal Distribution — Shortcuts

Only use shortcuts **after** you understand the underlying concepts in `NOTES.md`.

### Shortcut: 68–95–99.7

**Why it works:** ±1σ≈68%, ±2σ≈95%, ±3σ≈99.7%

**When to use:** Quick estimates

**Example:** σ=10, μ=70: 60–80≈68%

**Trap / limitation:** Approximate only
