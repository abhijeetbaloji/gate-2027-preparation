# Normal Distribution — Mistakes

## Common Mistakes

These are **general/common traps** — not personal error logs.

### General/common trap: σ vs σ²

**What goes wrong:** Using variance as SD in Z=(X−μ)/σ

**Correct rule:** σ=√σ²

**Prevention:** Identify parameter from notation

---

### General/common trap: Sum of SDs

**What goes wrong:** SD(X+Y)=SD(X)+SD(Y)

**Correct rule:** SD=√(σ₁²+σ₂²)

**Prevention:** Variances add, SDs don't

---

### General/common trap: P(X=x) for continuous

**What goes wrong:** Non-zero point probability

**Correct rule:** P(X=x)=0

**Prevention:** Use P(X≤x) or intervals
