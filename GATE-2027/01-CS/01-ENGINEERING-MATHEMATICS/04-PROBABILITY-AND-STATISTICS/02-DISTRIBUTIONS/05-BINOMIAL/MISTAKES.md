# Binomial Distribution — Mistakes

## Common Mistakes

These are **general/common traps** — not personal error logs.

### General/common trap: Independence

**What goes wrong:** Using binomial without replacement

**Correct rule:** Need fixed p, independent

**Prevention:** Check sampling method

---

### General/common trap: Poisson too early

**What goes wrong:** Approximating when p large

**Correct rule:** Need p small

**Prevention:** p=0.5,n=10: use exact

---

### General/common trap: Mode formula

**What goes wrong:** Using np instead of ⌊(n+1)p⌋

**Correct rule:** Mode≈⌊(n+1)p⌋

**Prevention:** Check boundary ties
