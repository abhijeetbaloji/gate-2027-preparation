# Median — Formulas

Reference only. Learn concepts in `NOTES.md` first.

| Formula | Meaning | Variables | Conditions | Derivation | Typical GATE use | Common mistake |
|---------|---------|-----------|------------|------------|------------------|----------------|
| Odd n | value at (n+1)/2 | Sorted data | — | Middle position | Raw data | Forgetting sort |
| Even n | (x_{n/2}+x_{n/2+1})/2 | Sorted data | — | Two middles | Even count | Single middle only |
| Continuous | F(m)=0.5 | — | — | CDF half | Uniform, Normal | P(X=m) for continuous |
