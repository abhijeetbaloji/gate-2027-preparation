# Median — Mistakes

## Common Mistakes

These are **general/common traps** — not personal error logs.

### General/common trap: No sort

**What goes wrong:** Taking middle of unsorted data

**Correct rule:** Sort ascending first

**Prevention:** Always order

---

### General/common trap: Even n single middle

**What goes wrong:** Using one middle value only

**Correct rule:** Average two middles

**Prevention:** n=4: avg of 2nd and 3rd

---

### General/common trap: Mean vs median

**What goes wrong:** Using mean for skewed data

**Correct rule:** Median robust

**Prevention:** Compare both
