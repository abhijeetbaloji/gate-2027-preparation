# Standard Deviation — Quick Revision

- **Var(X) = E[X²] − (E[X])²**; **σ = √Var**.
- **Var(aX+b) = a²Var**; **SD(aX+b) = |a|σ**.
- **Independent:** Var(X+Y)=Var(X)+Var(Y); SD(X+Y)=√(σ_X²+σ_Y²).
- **Bin:** np(1−p); **Poisson:** λ; **Uniform [a,b]:** (b−a)²/12.
- **Trap:** σ vs σ² in N(μ,σ²); SD sum ≠ sum of SDs.
