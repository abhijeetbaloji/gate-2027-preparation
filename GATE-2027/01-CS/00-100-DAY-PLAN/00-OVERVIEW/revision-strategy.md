# Revision strategy

Revision is part of the day, not a reward for finishing the syllabus.

## Ladder

1. **Same topic, same day.** Practice and PYQ on the clause just studied.
2. **Next day.** About 25 minutes on the clause opened the day before.
3. **Older return.** About 20 minutes on a clause that is already open and is due on the rotation written in that day's `revision-target.md`.
4. **Weekly paper.** The week's clauses plus a cumulative share from earlier weeks. The share grows later in the plan.
5. **Monthly paper.** Everything studied up to that paper.
6. **Phase 4 sweeps.** Whole sections, in the pairs named on Days 85, 87, 89, and 90.
7. **Phase 5–6.** The tracker and the mistake book choose the clauses. Default rotations exist only when the tracker is still blank, and they are labelled as defaults.

## Rules

- A section does not drop out after its first-study days. If a day file names it, do that return even when the new clause is more interesting.
- Consolidation days (6, 20, 34, 48, 62, 76, 90) contain no new clause. They exist so the weekly or monthly paper is not the first time you see the week's work together.
- Do not build a second set of subject notes inside the day folder. The revision log is a checklist and a pointer into the mistake book.
- When a return feels solid, still do the minutes. Use them on a harder missed item, not on a new heading.
- When a return collapses, the next paper's cumulative slice should include that clause again. Write that instruction into the weekly tracker.

## What "revised" means

A checkbox in `subject-coverage.md` for a later return means you solved or re-solved at least one item and logged the result. Rereading a heading is not a revision.

## Mistake book

Each entry:

- Day number
- Clause, using the official wording
- Error type: concept, setup, arithmetic, misread, or guess
- Trigger: the check that would have caught it
- Open or closed

Every non-test day, review the two oldest open entries. Every analysis day, close an entry only after a clean redo without looking at the fix.

## Final ten days

Priority order: open mistakes, weak clauses from MOCK-03 and MOCK-04, General Aptitude, then a fast pass over official headings that have not appeared in the last week. New clauses are out of bounds.
