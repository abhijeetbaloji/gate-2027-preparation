# Test strategy

Every scheduled paper is 100 marks. This folder does not contain questions.

## Paper types

| Type | Ids | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alternate-day | TEST-001–TEST-026 | Mostly the latest study, plus a cumulative share that grows over the 100 days |
| Weekly | WEEKLY-01–WEEKLY-10 | The week, plus earlier weeks. Not a reprint of the last seven days |
| Monthly | MONTHLY-01–MONTHLY-09 | Everything studied up to that paper |
| Full mock | MOCK-01–MOCK-04 | Unseen archive paper, 180 minutes, exam rules |

Weeks 3, 7, 10, and 13 are closed by MONTHLY-02, MONTHLY-05, MONTHLY-07, and MONTHLY-09. A second paper is not added on those days.

## When the paper falls

- Alternate papers are on even days that are not a consolidation day, a weekly day, a monthly day, a mock day, or Day 92.
- Consolidation days with no paper: 6, 20, 34, 48, 62, 76, 90. Each one prepares the paper on the following day.
- Day 92 analyses MONTHLY-09 and does not host TEST-0xx. Stacking a second 100-mark paper on a 180-minute analysis is rejected as an impossible load.
- Days 87, 89, 95, 97, and 99 analyse the previous paper and revise. They do not add a paper.
- Day 100 is TEST-026, 90 minutes, mixed. The rest of the day is revision.

## Mix

| Stage | Recent clauses | Older clauses already studied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alternate papers in Days 2–28 | about 80 marks | about 20 marks |
| Alternate papers in Days 32–60 | about 60 | about 40 |
| Alternate papers from Day 64 through Day 82 | about 40 | about 60 |
| TEST-026 | about 30 from the last sweeps | about 70 full syllabus, including GA |
| WEEKLY-01 to WEEKLY-03 | about 60 this week | about 40 earlier |
| WEEKLY-04 to WEEKLY-07 | about 45 | about 55 |
| WEEKLY-09 and WEEKLY-10 | about 30 | about 70 |
| Every monthly paper | 0 as a separate "new" block | 100 cumulative |

MONTHLY-09 and every full mock use the official shape: General Aptitude 15, Engineering Mathematics 13, remaining subject questions 72. Earlier monthly papers cover only what has been studied and do not pretend to that split.

## How to sit

Alternate, weekly, and monthly papers:

- Assemble the paper before the day, from clauses named in that day's `test-target.md`, using PYQ items you have not already solved open-book.
- If you cannot assemble a full 100 marks, sit a shorter paper and record the mark total you actually sat. Do not invent items in this folder.
- One sitting, no notes, no pause except a stretch that stays inside the time.
- Score MCQs with the official penalties. Leave blanks at zero. MSQ and NAT: all or nothing, no penalty.

Full mocks:

- Open the named PDF only when the clock starts.
- 180 minutes. Virtual calculator only, as in the exam-pattern notes. No phone, no solution key beside you.
- GA, Engineering Mathematics, and the rest of CS will appear as that paper printed them. Do not rebalance the paper.
- Log the score at night. Classify misses the next day.

Suggested section order in a full mock, once you have a reason to change it: your own data from MOCK-01. Until then, start with the questions you can settle, mark the ones that need a calculation, and do not camp on a single item.

## Analysis block

For each miss or lucky guess:

- Clause
- Error type
- Whether negative marking made the guess expensive
- The smallest fact or check that would have changed the result

Then write two clauses into the next day's note. The following older-return block should use those clauses.
