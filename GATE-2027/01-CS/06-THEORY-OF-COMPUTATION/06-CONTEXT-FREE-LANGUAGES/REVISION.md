# Context-Free Languages — Revision

- CFL = CFG = NPDA.
- Closed: ∪, concat, star, reverse, homomorphism, ∩ regular, `L − R` (`R` regular).
- Not closed: ∩ of two CFLs, complement, CFL − CFL.
- Nested / sequential pairs: CFL. Crossed pairs / three equal counts: not.
- `{ww^R}` CFL; `{ww}` not. `{wcw^R}` DCFL.
- Both `L` and `L̄` can be CFL while `L` is not regular (`{a^n b^n}` over `{a,b}`).
- Regular ⊂ DCFL ⊂ CFL ⊂ decidable.
- `P` regular, `Q ⊆ P`: `Σ* − P` is always regular; `Q` need not be.
